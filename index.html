Features

Everything you need for currency & calculation

All financial tools in one compact Chrome extension — always at your fingertips.

Instant Currency Conversion

Convert between 170+ international currencies with real-time exchange rates. From USD, EUR, GBP, JPY to rare and exotic currencies — all in one place.

Multi-Currency Conversion

Enter one amount and instantly see conversions to up to 9 currencies simultaneously. Perfect for forex traders, international shoppers, and frequent travelers.

Built-in Scientific Calculator

A full scientific calculator with sin, cos, tan, log, ln, square root, exponents, and more. Results are automatically converted to your selected currency.

Smart History & Favorites

All conversions and calculations are saved automatically. Bookmark your most-used currency pairs for instant one-tap access.

55 Languages Supported

Switch the interface language in-app instantly — no reinstall needed. English, Vietnamese, Chinese, Japanese, Arabic, Hindi, and 49 more.

Chrome Side Panel

Lives in Chrome's built-in Side Panel — always visible alongside any webpage. Convert rates without switching tabs or opening a popup.

Quick Start

Up and running in 3 steps

No account, no sign-up. Install and start converting immediately.

Install the Extension

Click "Add to Chrome" in the Chrome Web Store. The extension installs in seconds — no account or registration required.

Open the Side Panel

Click the FX Convert & Calc icon in your Chrome toolbar to open the Side Panel. It stays visible alongside any webpage you browse.

Convert & Calculate

Enter any amount, choose your currencies, and get instant results. Use the calculator tab to perform expressions with automatic currency conversion.
