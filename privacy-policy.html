1Overview

Short version: FX Convert & Calc does not collect or transmit any personal data. Your preferences (currency choices, history, wallpaper) are stored locally on your device only. The only external request is to fetch live exchange rates.

FX Convert & Calc ("the Extension", "we", "our") is a Chrome browser extension that provides currency conversion, exchange rate lookups, and a scientific calculator within the Google Chrome Side Panel. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with your use of the Extension.

By installing and using FX Convert & Calc, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.

2Information We Collect

The Extension stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Currency Preferences: Your selected "from" and "to" currencies and your custom multi-currency list — saved so your settings persist between sessions.
  • Conversion History: A local log of recent conversions and calculator results for quick reference. Stored only on your device, never transmitted.
  • Favorite Currency Pairs: Currency pairs you have bookmarked for one-tap access.
  • Interface Preferences: Your selected language, wallpaper/background choice, and active tab — saved locally for a consistent experience.

All data above is stored exclusively in Chrome's chrome.storage.local, accessible only to this Extension on your device.

3Information We Do NOT Collect

We explicitly do not collect, transmit, or store any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, or any personal identity information
  • Browsing history, visited URLs, or web page content
  • Device identifiers, IP addresses, or location data
  • Analytics, crash reports, or usage telemetry
  • Advertising identifiers or tracking cookies
No account is required. We never know who you are or how you use the Extension.

4How We Use Your Information

The locally stored data is used solely to:

  • Remember your currency selections and preferences between sessions
  • Display your conversion history and favorites within the Extension
  • Restore your preferred interface language and appearance

We do not use any data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond the core functionality of the Extension.

5Data Storage & Security

Local Storage Only: All user preferences are stored in chrome.storage.local, isolated to your browser profile and inaccessible to other extensions or websites.

No Server Storage: We do not operate any database or server that stores user data. There is no account system, no cloud sync, and no user profile associated with your use of the Extension.

Data Removal: All locally stored data (history, preferences, favorites) is permanently deleted when you uninstall the Extension. You can also manually clear history and reset preferences from within the Extension at any time.

6Exchange Rate API

To provide live exchange rates, the Extension makes requests to our exchange rate API endpoint. These requests contain only the currency codes being queried (e.g., "USD", "EUR") — no user identifiers, device information, or personal data are included in these requests.

The exchange rate data returned is displayed to you in the Extension. It is not stored permanently beyond your session history preference.

Example request: POST /api/currency/conversion with { "from": "USD", "to": "VND", "amout": 100 }. No personal data is sent.

7Third-Party Services

FX Convert & Calc does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, social media, or data broker services.

The Extension uses the Google Chrome extension platform and is distributed through the Google Chrome Web Store. Your use is subject to Google's Privacy Policy where applicable to Chrome browser functionality.
